NEET PG Rank Analysis

An Android app by Lunvex

NEET PG Rank Analysis helps NEET-PG candidates in India plan their MCC counselling choices using official past allotment data. All data comes from public documents published by the Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) and NBEMS, and is bundled in the app so it works offline.

Get it on Google Play

Screenshots

What the app does

  • Look up any NEET-PG rank and see which colleges and branches candidates near that rank were allotted.
  • Browse 2025 allotments for Round 1, Round 2, Round 3 and the Stray Vacancy round, by state, college, department, quota and category.
  • See the seat matrix across AIQ, Central, Deemed and DNB seats.
  • Read plain-language guides to course types, quotas and categories.
  • Practise with a mock counselling walkthrough before the real process.

Free and paid features

Round 1 data and a preview of the Round 2 walkthrough are free. Full access to every round and all analysis features is a one-time purchase of ₹99 — see Pricing.
