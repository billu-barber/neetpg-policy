Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy explains what information the NEET PG Rank Analysis app ("the App") collects and how it is used. The App is published by Lunvex (contact: theeighteyed@gmail.com).

Information we collect

  • Name and email address — collected when you sign in, so the App can identify your account.
  • Purchase information — if you buy the full-access unlock on our website, our payment processor Razorpay tells us the payment ID, amount and the email you entered, which we use to mark your account as unlocked. We do not see or store your card, UPI or bank details; those are handled entirely by Razorpay.

All NEET-PG counselling data (seat matrix, allotment results, etc.) shown in the App is stored locally on your device and is not linked to your personal information.

How we use this information

Solely to let you sign in, remember your account, and unlock paid features you purchase. We do not use your data for advertising, and the App shows no ads.

How we store and protect it

Your account and purchase-status data is stored securely in Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Access is restricted so that only your own signed-in account can read your data.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, except Google (Firebase), which we use to operate sign-in, and Razorpay, which processes website payments.

Data deletion

You can delete your account at any time from within the App (Account → Delete account), or by emailing us at theeighteyed@gmail.com from your account email.

What is deleted: your sign-in, name, email address, and your unlock and progress records on our server. This happens immediately when you delete in the App, or within 7 days of an email request.

What is kept: a record of any payment made on this website (your email, the payment ID and the amount) is kept as a transaction record, as payment and tax record-keeping requires. If you later sign in again with the same Google account, that purchase is restored.

Children

The App is intended for medical exam aspirants and is not directed at children under 13.
