Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

When you get a full refund

Full access to NEET PG Rank Analysis is a digital product that unlocks in the app as soon as payment is confirmed. You get a full refund if:

  • you paid, but the app did not unlock after you signed in with the same email you entered when paying, and we cannot fix it; or
  • you were charged more than once for the same purchase (the extra payments are refunded).

How to ask

Email us at theeighteyed@gmail.com within 7 days of paying, with the email you paid with and the payment ID from your payment receipt. We check our payment records, usually within 2 working days. If the app did not unlock, we first try to unlock it; if we cannot, we refund the full amount.

Refunds go back to the original payment method (UPI, card or bank account) through our payment processor, Razorpay. Your bank may take a few working days to show it.

When there is no refund

Once the app has unlocked, the purchase is final: there is no refund for change of mind or for not using the app.

Cancellation

Full access is a one-time payment, not a subscription. Nothing renews automatically and there is nothing to cancel. Access ends on its own when the 2027 NEET-PG result is declared.
